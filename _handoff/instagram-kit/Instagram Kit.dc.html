Post 2 · Who we are — text card
We don't really do small talk. It's hard to, when you're side by side on a trail instead of face to face across a table — somehow the good stuff just comes out.

Sisi Club is built around movement and the outdoors: long walks in the Alps, yoga in the meadow, a matcha at the top. No fees, no pressure, no one left behind. 🏔️
#sisiclubgeneva #girlswhohike #genève #slowliving #womenofswitzerland

Our namesake
Named for an empress who never sat still.
Empress Sisi walked for hours every day, a hound always at her heel. We borrowed her name, her walking shoes, and her belief that a long walk fixes almost anything.
Elisabeth of Austria · 1837–1898
Post 4 · The Sisi story — text card
Meet our namesake. Empress Elisabeth of Austria — Sisi — walked for hours every single day, often so far and so fast her ladies-in-waiting begged for mercy. She was never without a hound at her heel.

We borrowed her name, her walking shoes, and her firm belief that a long walk fixes almost anything. 🤍
#empresssisi #sisiclub #inSisisSpirit #womeninnature #swissalps
